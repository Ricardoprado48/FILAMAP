## 1. Em 2026 e 2027, Simples Nacional/MEI precisam preencher os grupos IBS/CBS em NF-e/NFC-e/NFS-e?

### Takeaway
No for 2026: Simples Nacional (CRT 1), sublimit excess (CRT 2) and MEI (CRT 4) are exempt from the IBSCBS group all year, on both NF-e/NFC-e and NFS-e. Yes from January 2027: the NT 2025.002 sets the IBSCBS group as mandatory for CRT 1 in production from 04/01/2027, including CST and cClassTrib. The detailed rules for how CRT 1 fills the group are still waiting on a "future NT", so the exact layout for the conventional Simples in 2027 is not final.

### Cited Findings
- NT 2025.002 (v1.33), rule UB12-10: the IBSCBS group is filled by Simples Nacional issuers (CRT 1) only from 01/2027. In production for CRT=1 from 04/01/2027. Guidance specific to CRT=1 "to be published in a future NT", because IBS/CBS/IS taxation for these taxpayers only starts in 2027 (snippet) — [Tecnospeed blog](https://blog.tecnospeed.com.br/nota-tecnica-reforma-tributaria-nfe-nfce/); [Inventti NT 2025.002 v1.30](https://inventti.com.br/reforma-tributaria-nf-e-nfc-e-nt-2025-002-v1-30-novo-cronograma-e-alteracoes-importantes/); [Flexdocs guia NT 2025/002, atualizado 10-09-26](https://flexdocs.net/guiaNFe/guiaAtualizacao.RT2025002.html)
- The NT states that "para os optantes do Simples Nacional, os grupos IBSCBS só serão obrigatórios a partir de 2027". The group is not required when CRT = 1 (Simples) or 4 (MEI). For CRT 1, 2 and 4 the obligation enters production on 04/01/2027 (snippet) — [JBSoft "Simples Nacional: nada muda em 2026"](https://blog.jbsoft.com.br/simples-nacional-nada-muda-em-2026/); [MRS Advogados, NT 2025.002 v1.51](https://mrsadvogados.com/nota-tecnica-2025-002-versao-1-51-destaque-ibs-e-cbs-na-nota-fiscal-eletronica-nf-e-e-na-nota-fiscal-de-consumidor-eletronica-nfc-e/)
- Sending the group as CRT 1 in 2026 "could result in rejection or the group being ignored" (snippet) — [Jettax](https://www.jettax.com.br/blog/simples-nacional-e-mei-ibs-e-cbs-na-nota-fiscal-em-2026-entenda-as-regras/). **Conflicts with** the next finding, which says Simples companies may fill the fields voluntarily in 2026 (snippet) — [SpedBrasil](https://spedbrasil.com.br/simples-nacional-ibs-cbs-notas-fiscais-2027/); [Contábeis 78641](https://www.contabeis.com.br/noticias/78641/simples-nacional-ibs-cbs-em-notas-fiscais-so-a-partir-de-2027/). **Flag:** the two sources disagree on voluntary filling.
- NFS-e: in 2026, Simples Nacional and MEI are dispensed from reporting IBS/CBS. Until 31/12/2026, missing IBS/CBS will not cause the national system to reject the NFS-e (snippet) — [Contábeis "NFS-e: veja prazos para informar IBS e CBS em 2026"](https://www.contabeis.com.br/noticias/78630/nfs-e-veja-prazos-para-informar-ibs-e-cbs-em-2026/); [Contábeis "NFS-e sem IBS e CBS: entenda a desconformidade"](https://www.contabeis.com.br/noticias/79499/nfs-e-sem-ibs-e-cbs-entenda-a-desconformidade/)
- NFS-e: Simples Nacional ME/EPP must use the Emissor Nacional exclusively from 01/11/2026, whatever the IBS/CBS timetable (snippet) — [Jettax](https://www.jettax.com.br/blog/simples-nacional-e-mei-ibs-e-cbs-na-nota-fiscal-em-2026-entenda-as-regras/)
- 2027 new fields for Simples: the CST of the new taxes and the cClassTrib. The two must be compatible with each other. When the buyer has a right to credit, the data in the document is the reference for the IBS/CBS credit, so correcting a note also affects the other party (snippet) — [SpedBrasil](https://spedbrasil.com.br/simples-nacional-ibs-cbs-notas-fiscais-2027/); [Ocana Contabilidade](https://ocana.com.br/empresas-do-simples-precisam-preparar-sistemas-para-novas-regras-das-notas-fiscais-em-2027/)
- Ato Conjunto RFB/CGIBS nº 1 of 22/12/2025 (DOU 23/12/2025) sets the accessory obligations for 2026. It requires taxpayers subject to IBS/CBS to issue electronic fiscal documents. It sets no penalties for missing IBS/CBS fields until the 1st day of the 4th month after publication of the common part of the regulations (snippet) — [Normas Legais](https://www.normaslegais.com.br/legislacao/ato-conjunto-rfb-cgibs-1-2025.htm); [Felsberg](https://www.felsberg.com.br/publicado-o-ato-conjunto-rfb-cgibs-no-1-2025-o-qual-dispoe-sobre-as-obrigacoes-acessorias-para-ibs-e-cbs-em-2026/); [Sovos](https://sovos.com/pt-br/atualizacoes-regulatorias/tributos/brasil-ato-conjunto-rfb-cgibs-no-1-2025-esclarece-documentos-fiscais-eletronicos-e-penalidades-de-ibs-e-cbs-em-2026/)

### Inferences
- The Simples/MEI base (about 23.6M CNPJs) has no filling obligation on NF-e, NFC-e or NFS-e in 2026. A 2026 "conferência de classificação" offer aimed at Simples-issued notes has essentially no compliance pain behind it. The pain for these issuers starts in January 2027.
- From 2027, even the conventional Simples (tax paid inside the DAS) must send CST/cClassTrib. The buyer's credit depends on those notes, so misclassification by a Simples supplier becomes a problem for its regime-normal clients.
- The specific CRT=1 rules are still "future NT". Any product built for Simples 2027 notes is working against a moving spec.

### Gaps
- I could not read the literal text of NT 2025.002 UB12-10, or confirm the latest NT version as of Sept 2026. Flexdocs lists an update dated 10-09-26, but I could not open it.
- It is unclear whether the "future NT" for CRT 1 has been published. If it has, I could not find which CST/cClassTrib combinations a conventional Simples issuer uses in 2027.
- The NFS-e rules for Simples in 2027 (national layout fields) were not confirmed from a primary source.

## 2. Regime normal (Lucro Presumido/Real): which fields are mandatory, since when, and what penalties apply?

### Takeaway
For regime normal (CRT 3), filling the IBS/CBS fields in NF-e/NFC-e has been a legal obligation since 1/1/2026, with test rates of CBS 0.9% and IBS 0.1%. Payment is waived if the accessory obligations are met. The Aug/2026 milestone:
- **Penalties:** the grace period ended on 1/08/2026, because the regulations were published on 30/04/2026.
- **Rejection:** validation-based rejection was due on 03/08/2026. On 31/07/2026 the Ato Técnico Conjunto CGIBS/RFB nº 1 suspended it, with no new date. The notes are not blocked, but filling them in remains mandatory.

### Cited Findings
- 2026 test rates: CBS 0.9% plus IBS 0.1%. ADCT art. 125 §4 waives payment of IBS/CBS for taxable events from 1/1 to 31/12/2026 if the accessory obligations are fulfilled. LC 214/2025 also provides for offset against PIS/Cofins (snippet) — [TaxGroup](https://www.taxgroup.com.br/solutions/aliquota-teste-de-ibs-e-cbs-guia-completo-de-como-vai-funcionar/); [CRC-SC PDF](https://www.crcsc.org.br/uploads/pagina/14063/M7iXZjxLqEoTDqhIRuBh9HHEM1s65rcj.pdf)
- The regulations were published on 30/04/2026: Decreto nº 12.955/2026 (CBS) and Resolução CGIBS nº 6/2026 (IBS). Counting 4 months from publication, penalties start on 01/08/2026. Some sources describe 2026 penalties as "educational" in character (snippet) — [Inventti](https://inventti.com.br/ibs-e-cbs-regulamentados-prazo-para-penalidades-comeca-em-agosto-de-2026/); [Contábeis "marco de 1º de agosto de 2026"](https://www.contabeis.com.br/artigos/76490/reforma-tributaria-marco-de-1o-de-agosto-de-2026-detalhado/); [APET: regulation provides fines](https://apet.org.br/noticia/regulamento-do-cbs-e-ibs-preve-multa-para-contribuinte-que-nao-cumprir-obrigacoes/)
- Earlier coverage (Q1 2026) reported fines suspended "until March" or "until 1 April". This predates the regulations and reflects the "4 months after the regulation" rule being read before the regulation existed. **Flag:** it is outdated, not a conflict — [Menos Imposto substack](https://menosimposto.substack.com/p/ibs-e-cbs-em-2026-receita-suspende); [IRTDPJ Brasil](https://irtdpjbrasil.org.br/receita-suspende-ate-1o-de-abril-multas-por-notas-emitidas-sem-ibs-e-cbs); [Fenacon](https://fenacon.org.br/reforma-tributaria/multa-por-falta-de-cbs-e-ibs-em-notas-e-suspensa-no-inicio-de-2026/)
- For Lucro Real and Presumido, rejection for missing IBS/CBS fields was due in production from 03/08/2026. On 31/07/2026 the Ato Técnico Conjunto CGIBS/RFB nº 1 suspended the validation rules that would make filling mandatory for authorization (snippet) — [Contábeis "Rejeição 1115 adiada, mas obrigação permanece"](https://www.contabeis.com.br/noticias/79337/nf-e-rejeicao-1115-de-ibs-e-cbs-adiada-mas-obrigacao-permanece); [ERPWorks "caiu a rejeição, não a obrigação"](https://erpworks.com.br/conteudos/nfe-ibs-cbs-obrigatoriedade-agosto-2026-rejeicoes/)
- That Ato Técnico Conjunto (published in the DOU on 03/08/2026) covers 8 documents: NF-e, NFC-e, CT-e, CT-e OS, GTV-e, BP-e, NF3e and NFCom. The "obligation initiated on August 3 continues in effect". The rejection was postponed with no new date (snippet) — [IOB Notícias](https://noticias.iob.com.br/reforma-adiamento-rejeicao-notas-ibs-cbs/); [SpaceMoney](https://www.spacemoney.com.br/economia/legislacao/prazo-extra-ibs-cbs-nota); [CGIBS atos técnicos conjuntos](https://www.cgibs.gov.br/atos-tecnicos-conjuntos); [reformatributaria.com](https://www.reformatributaria.com/documentos-fiscais/receita-e-comite-gestor-suspende-rejeicao-de-notas-fiscais-de-documentos-fiscais-saiba-quais)
- NFS-e for regime normal: coverage speaks of a "desconformidade" for NFS-e issued without IBS/CBS. I could not read the full article (snippet, title only) — [Contábeis 79499](https://www.contabeis.com.br/noticias/79499/nfs-e-sem-ibs-e-cbs-entenda-a-desconformidade/)
- Mandatory fields named across sources: CST IBS/CBS, cClassTrib, base, rates and values of the IBS UF, IBS Mun and CBS (snippet) — [Contmatic "campos obrigatórios agosto 2026"](https://simplifique.contmatic.com.br/blogs/ibs-cbs-nfe-campos-obrigatorios-agosto-2026); [Contmatic "Tabelas de Classificação e CST"](https://simplifique.contmatic.com.br/blogs/ibs-e-cbs-entenda-as-tabelas-de-classificacao-e-cst)

### Inferences
- Since the suspension of the rejection, the SEFAZ no longer catches wrong or missing classification at authorization. Errors pass through silently while the legal obligation and possible fines (from 01/08/2026) stay in place. That is exactly the gap an after-the-fact XML audit service fills, today, for Lucro Presumido/Real clients.
- 2026 has a concrete financial stake beyond fines: the waiver of the 0.9% + 0.1% payment is conditional on meeting the accessory obligations.

### Gaps
- I did not find the fine amounts (per document or percentage) in Decreto 12.955/2026 or Resolução CGIBS 6/2026. APET says the regulation "prevê multa", but I have no amount.
- It is unclear whether the ADCT waiver would actually be lost because of classification errors, as opposed to total omission. No enforcement guidance was found.
- I found no primary text for the NFS-e "desconformidade" rule for regime normal.

## 3. Simples híbrido (IBS/CBS pelo regime regular): rules, windows, who opts, and how many

### Takeaway
Resolução CGSN nº 186/2026 (April 2026) created the option. The window, 1-30/09/2026, was extended by Resolução CGSN nº 194/2026 (DOU 28/09/2026):
- **Hybrid option:** until 30/10/2026.
- **Simples entry option:** until 15/10/2026.
- **Cancellation:** from 03/11 to 20/12/2026.

The option is semiannual: a September choice covers Jan-Jun/2027, and the next window, in March 2027, covers H2/2027. By 28-29/09/2026 there were 586,675 requests, about 8.3% of Simples companies, almost all in the final two weeks.

### Cited Findings
- The Simples option for 2027 was exceptionally brought forward to 1-30/09/2026. In the same window, optantes could choose to pay IBS/CBS under the regime regular from 01/01/2027. In the "Simples híbrido", CBS/IBS leave the DAS and are calculated under their own rules. ## 5. Do regime-normal buyers suffer from Simples suppliers' notes (credit chain)?

### Takeaway
Yes. Under LC 214/2025 art. 47 §9, a regime-normal buyer can take IBS/CBS credit on purchases from a conventional Simples supplier. The credit is limited to the IBS/CBS portion actually due inside the DAS, not the full rate. Hybrid suppliers transfer full credit.

The buyer's credit is determined from the fiscal document, so from 2027 the CST/cClassTrib and values on Simples-issued notes directly drive the client's recoverable credit. That is the B2B pain: a regime-normal client of an accounting firm needs its inbound Simples notes classified correctly.

### Cited Findings
- LC 214/2025 art. 47 §9:
  - Item I: the Simples optante cannot take IBS/CBS credits on its own purchases.
  - Item II: a regime-regular buyer may take credit for the IBS/CBS paid on purchases from an optante, limited to the amount due through the Simples Nacional.
  - The credit does not equal the regular rate applied to the operation value. It equals the portion actually due by the supplier in the Simples.
  (snippet) — [Migalhas 461774](https://www.migalhas.com.br/depeso/461774/credito-de-ibs-e-cbs-a-nao-cumulatividade-que-depende-do-fornecedor); [Migalhas 463764 / Fenacon](https://fenacon.org.br/reforma-tributaria/simples-nacional-e-ibs-cbs-impactos-para-fornecedor-e-cliente/); [LC 214/2025 — Planalto](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/lcp/lcp214.htm) (primary, not fetched)
- Even when a Simples supplier does not highlight IBS/CBS, the regime-normal buyer may take credit equal to the IBS/CBS portion embedded in the supplier's DAS (snippet) — [Simtax](https://simtax.com.br/credito-ibs-cbs-simples-nacional/)
- The Simples híbrido option calculates IBS/CBS under the general LC 214 rules, meaning full credit to buyers (snippet) — [Migalhas / Fenacon](https://fenacon.org.br/reforma-tributaria/simples-nacional-e-ibs-cbs-impactos-para-fornecedor-e-cliente/); [ConJur](https://conjur.com.br/2026-set-08/simples-nacional-e-ibs-cbs-quando-apurar-por-fora-do-das-cria-valor/)
- When there is a right to credit, the fiscal document data is the reference for credit use. Correcting a note affects the counterparty (snippet) — [SpedBrasil](https://spedbrasil.com.br/simples-nacional-ibs-cbs-notas-fiscais-2027/)
- There is a debate on credit when a Simples company is audited or reassessed (title only) — [ConJur 09/06/2026](https://conjur.com.br/2026-jun-09/o-direito-ao-credito-do-ibs-cbs-nas-autuacoes-das-empresas-enquadradas-no-simples-nacional-2/)

### Inferences
- Classification pain splits into two directions:
  1. **Outbound:** regime-normal and hybrid issuers must classify their own notes correctly (obligation, fines, and the 2026 payment waiver).
  2. **Inbound:** regime-normal buyers must check the CST/cClassTrib and values on received notes, including Simples-issued ones from 2027, to know what credit they can take.
- For an accounting firm, inbound audit on regime-normal clients may be the stronger pitch in 2027, because it touches money (credit) rather than only compliance.
- In 2026 inbound notes from Simples carry no IBS/CBS group, so there is nothing to audit on them yet. Inbound auditing of regime-normal suppliers' notes is already relevant, though 2026 credits are notional (test rate, offset against PIS/Cofins).

### Gaps
- I did not verify the exact article and paragraph numbering in the Planalto LC 214 text; the proxy blocked the fetch.
- It is unclear how a conventional Simples note will carry the "credit value" field in 2027. This depends on the pending CRT 1 NT.

## 6. How are accounting firms' client portfolios split between Simples and regime normal?

### Takeaway
I found no reliable quantitative survey of accounting firms' portfolio split by tax regime. Qualitative evidence says most small firms serve mainly Simples, MEI and Lucro Presumido, and that firms are small: 28% have up to 20 clients and 20% have 20-50.

### Cited Findings
- "28% dos escritórios têm uma carteira de até 20 clientes e 20% atendem de 20 a 50". Most accountants surveyed serve Simples Nacional, Lucro Presumido and MEI clients (snippet) — [Contábeis "Pesquisa revela perfil do mercado contábil"](https://www.contabeis.com.br/noticias/60322/pesquisa-revela-perfil-do-mercado-contabil-e-escritorios-no-pais/)
- SESCON-SP publishes a "Pesquisa de preços e serviços praticados pelas empresas contábeis" (PDF, 2024 errata). It may contain a regime breakdown, but it was blocked (not read) — [SESCON PDF](https://sescon.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/PESQUISA-PRECOS-E-SERVICOS-ERRATA.pdf)

### Inferences
- If a typical firm's portfolio mirrors the national company mix (excluding MEI, roughly 80% Simples and 20% Presumido/Real), a 50-client firm has about 10 regime-normal clients with real pain in 2026. This is a proxy only, not survey data. From 2027 it adds perhaps 3-4 hybrids (8% of about 40 Simples clients), plus inbound-credit work for the regime-normal clients.

### Gaps
- There is no survey from CFC, Fenacon, SESCON or ERP vendors (Domínio/Thomson Reuters, Questor, Alterdata, Nibo) giving the split of clients by regime. This is the key open data point for sizing, and a short primary survey of target firms is recommended.
