> Status as of 2026-09-30. NOT legal advice. ## 1. Is "revisão/conferência de documentos fiscais" / auditoria fiscal / consultoria tributária privativa de contador? Is consultoria tributária open to non-accountants?

### Takeaway
The statute (DL 9.295/1946, art. 25) and CFC Res. 1.640/2021 reserve to CRC-registered contadores things like fiscal bookkeeping, "revisão permanente ou periódica de escritas", auditing/perícia and technical responsibility for escrituração fiscal and apuração; but "assessoria e consultoria tributária" is expressly listed by the CFC itself as a **shared** activity (compartilhada com outras profissões). A tool or report that flags CST/cClassTrib inconsistencies in XMLs sits in a gray zone: a pure software feature that the client's own contador uses is low-risk; a done-for-you "auditoria fiscal" report signed by a non-contador is the higher-risk format. Note the OAB also claims "consultoria jurídica tributária" (interpreting law for a specific case) as privativa da advocacia.

### Cited Findings
- DL 9.295/1946, art. 25 defines "trabalhos técnicos de contabilidade": (a) organização e execução de serviços de contabilidade em geral; (b) escrituração dos livros obrigatórios e levantamento de balanços; (c) "perícias judiciais ou extrajudiciais, revisão de balanços e de contas em geral, verificação de haveres, revisão permanente ou periódica de escritas, regulações judiciais ou extrajudiciais de avarias grossas ou comuns, assistência aos Conselhos Fiscais das sociedades anônimas e quaisquer outras atribuições de natureza técnica conferidas por lei aos profissionais de contabilidade" (snippet) — [Câmara/DL 9.295 integral](https://www.camara.leg.br/proposicoesWeb/prop_mostrarintegra?codteor=2203074); [CFC PDF DL 9.295](https://cfc.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/decretolei_9295.pdf); [ModeloInicial art. 25](https://modeloinicial.com.br/lei/DEL-9295-1946/decreto-lei-9295/art-25)
- CFC FAQ: perícia and auditoria are "atribuições privativas dos contadores" not allowed to técnicos or "qualquer outro profissional de nível superior" (snippet) — [CFC – Prerrogativas FAQ](https://cfc.org.br/fiscalizacao-etica-e-disciplina/perguntas-frequentes/prerrogativas-de-contadores-e-tecnicos-em-contabilidade/); [CFC – Auditoria FAQ](https://cfc.org.br/fiscalizacao-etica-e-disciplina/perguntas-frequentes/auditoria/)

- OAB-SP has a parecer on "consultoria contábil e fiscal" (content not retrieved) — [OAB-SP PDF](https://www.oabsp.org.br/upload/525684856.pdf)
- The CFA (administradores) also claims "consultoria e assessoria empresarial e tributária" as administração activity in a sentence it publicizes — showing three councils claim overlapping turf (snippet) — [CFA sentença](https://cfa.org.br/sentenca-prestacao-de-servicos-de-consultoria-e-assessoria-empresarial-e-tributaria-atividade-relacionada-a-administracao-de-empresas/)

### Inferences
- A "conferência de CST/cClassTrib" in NF-e/NFS-e is closer to **tax compliance consulting** (shared under Res. 1.640 art. 5) than to escrituração fiscal or auditoria (privative). But labelling it "auditoria fiscal", "revisão da escrituração", or signing it as a technical opinion pushes it toward art. 25(c) "revisão permanente ou periódica de escritas" and toward the privative list. Wording/marketing matters: prefer "verificação automatizada de consistência", "alertas de classificação", "sugestão técnica sujeita à validação do contador responsável".
- Because the customer is the accounting firm itself (a CRC-registered organization with a responsible contador who decides and signs), the non-contador vendor is supplying an input/tool, not assuming technical responsibility for the client's escrituração. This is the strongest legal positioning.
- Suggesting "the correct cClassTrib is X because LC 214/2025 art. Y applies" is interpretation of law for a concrete case — theoretically arguable by the OAB as consultoria jurídica. Risk is low in practice for B2B tooling aimed at contadores, but a done-for-you report with legal reasoning is more exposed than software rules.
- Enforcement risk is primarily administrative (CRC fiscalização/multa to the entity, report to MP for LCP art. 47) and reputational, not criminal in practice.

### Gaps
- Could not fetch the full text of Res. 1.640/2021 (all CFC/legisweb domains blocked); exact inciso numbers for "revisão de obrigações acessórias"/"apuração de tributos" and whether they are in §1 (privativas de contador) vs technicians should be confirmed in the official PDF.
- Did not find any CFC normative text specifically addressing automated classification checking software or "conferência fiscal" by non-contadores.

## 2. Enforcement actions / court decisions against tax-tech or BPO fiscal without CRC; how tax-techs structure themselves

### Takeaway
CFC/CRCs actively fine "organização contábil sem registro" and "exploração de atividades contábeis sem registro", and forward exercício ilegal to the Ministério Público; but no decision was found against a pure software/tax-tech vendor. Market tax-techs (e-Auditoria, Sittax) position themselves as software sold **to** contadores; whether they hold CRC registration was not verifiable.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- The "software for contadores" model (the contador remains the decision-maker and signatory) is the dominant market structure and, apparently, not challenged by CFC/CRC. Services like credit recovery that tax-techs sell directly are usually performed with partner contadores/lawyers (not verified for Sittax).
- The enforcement pattern targets entities that **explore accounting activities** (bookkeeping, apuração, DP) without CRC registration — a vendor delivering reports to accounting firms is not the typical target, but a vendor selling "auditoria fiscal" directly to end companies would be.

## 3. Structures that make it legal: software vs service; partnership with contador; organização contábil registration; company formation (MEI, CNAEs)

### Takeaway
Safest: (a) SaaS licensed to accounting firms under a software CNAE (6201-5/01 is NOT MEI-eligible; 6311-9/00 / 6203-1/00 for SaaS), outputs framed as suggestions validated by the client's contador; (b) if selling done-for-you reports, either sub-contract under a partner contador's technical responsibility or form an organização contábil — which requires a contador as majority partner (Res. CFC 1.708/2023), so a lone developer cannot register one. Avoid CNAE 6920-6/01 and 6920-6/02, which are linked to the CRC.

### Cited Findings
- Res. CFC 1.708/2023 (revoked Res. 1.555/2018): PJs "constituídas para exploração das atividades contábeis, em qualquer modalidade, deverão ser registradas em CRC"; organizations must be integrated by contadores/técnicos, association with other regulated professionals allowed; the majority partner must be a profissional da contabilidade; S/A cannot register; registration requires "comprovação da responsabilidade técnica" (snippet) — [CRCMA notícia](https://crcma.org.br/noticias/resolucao-cfc-n-o-1-708-2023-define-criterios-para-sociedades-contabeis); [CFC Res. 1.708 PDF](https://www1.cfc.org.br/sisweb/SRE/docs/Res_1708.pdf); [CRC-ES legislação](https://crc-es.org.br/legislacao-contabil)
- Under the prior Res. 1.555/2018: registration granted when contabilidade is among the objects and contadores hold majority of capital; technical responsibility for privative services rests with the contador; other partners must be registered in their own councils (snippet) — [Contábeis Res. 1.555](https://www.contabeis.com.br/legislacao/4419469/resolucao-cfc-1555-2018/); [DOU Res. 1.555](https://www.in.gov.br/materia/-/asset_publisher/Kujrw0TZC2Mb/content/id/56125377/do1-2018-12-20-resolucao-n-1-555-de-6-de-dezembro-de-2018-56125273)
- NBC PG 01 (Código de Ética do Contador): the contador may transfer a contract to another professional with the client's written consent and "transferir parcialmente a execução dos serviços a seu cargo a outro contador, mantendo sempre como sua a responsabilidade técnica" (snippet) — [NBC PG 01 – DOU](https://www.in.gov.br/materia/-/asset_publisher/Kujrw0TZC2Mb/content/id/63361653/do1-2019-02-14-norma-brasileira-de-contabilidade-nbc-pg-01-de-7-de-fevereiro-de-2019-63361329); [Cosif NBC PG 01](https://cosif.com.br/publica.asp?arquivo=nbc-pg-01)
- CNAE 6201-5/01 (desenvolvimento de programas sob encomenda) is not on the MEI occupation list; developers need ME/EPP (e.g., SLU) — (snippet) — [Contabilidade.com](https://contabilidade.com/blog/desenvolvedor-pode-ser-mei-em-2026-veja-como-abrir-cnpj-escolher-o-cnae-ideal-e-pagar-menos-impostos/); [Econtrol](https://econtrolcontabilidade.com.br/desenvolvedor-de-software-pode-ser-mei-entenda-o-que-diz-a-legislacao-e-quais-alternativas-existem/); [Meu Contador Online – CNAEs software/MEI](https://www.meucontadoronline.com.br/consulta-cnae/cnae-desenvolvimento-de-software/)
- CNAE 6201-5/01 is subject to Fator R in Simples Nacional (snippet) — [Agilize](https://agilize.com.br/artigos/cnae-6201501-o-que-e/)
- CNAE 6920-6/01 = atividades de contabilidade (escrituração, balanços, declarações por contador); 6920-6/02 = consultoria e auditoria contábil e tributária, Anexo V with Fator R; 6920-6/02 is flagged with "Conselho de Classe: CRC" (snippet) — [IBGE CONCLA 6920-6/01](https://concla.ibge.gov.br/busca-online-cnae.html?subclasse=6920601&view=subclasse); [IBGE CONCLA 6920-6/02](https://concla.ibge.gov.br/busca-online-cnae.html?subclasse=6920602&tipo=cnae&view=subclasse); [Contabilizei 6920-6/02](https://www.contabilizei.com.br/consulta-cnae/cnae-atividades-juridicas-de-contabilidade-e-de-auditoria/6920602-atividades-de-consultoria-e-auditoria-contabil-e-tributaria/)

### Inferences
- Recommended base structure: Sociedade Limitada Unipessoal (SLU) / ME in Simples Nacional, primary CNAE 6311-9/00 (tratamento de dados, provedores de serviços de aplicação e hospedagem — typical for SaaS) or 6203-1/00 (software customizável não sob encomenda), secondary 6201-5/01 and possibly 6204-0/00 (consultoria em TI). Do NOT register 6920-6/01 or 6920-6/02: those CNAEs signal accounting activity and can trigger CRC registration demands. (CNAE choice to be confirmed with a contador; 6311/6203 descriptions not fetched here.)
- MEI is not viable: software CNAEs are not MEI occupations, and MEI cannot have a partner (so no contador partner either).
- For done-for-you reports: model 1 = "white-label" — the vendor produces automated findings and delivers them to the accounting firm, whose contador reviews and signs (no privative act by the vendor). Model 2 = partnership with a registered contador/organização contábil that assumes technical responsibility and invoices the "revisão"; the developer invoices the software/processing. Model 3 = own organização contábil — only possible with a contador as majority partner (Res. 1.708/2023), which dilutes control.

### Gaps
- Official IBGE descriptions of 6311-9/00 and 6203-1/00 and their MEI status not fetched; LC 214/2025 / Simples Nacional treatment of SaaS from 2027 not covered here.

## 4. Civil liability for errors; contracts, disclaimers, E&O insurance

### Takeaway
B2B limitation-of-liability caps are generally valid in Brazil (CC art. 421-A presumption of parity; STJ 2024 upheld a cap in a tech contract), but cannot exclude liability for dolo/culpa grave and are weaker if a court sees the accounting firm as hipossuficiente/consumer. Combine: contract cap (e.g., fees paid in last 12 months), explicit "sugestão técnica; decisão e responsabilidade técnica do contador do cliente", and E&O/RC Profissional insurance (offered in Brazil for tech companies by Allianz, Tokio Marine, Sompo, Porto, Akad, Fator; no public price found).

### Cited Findings
- Lei 13.874/2019 gave CC art. 421-A: civil and business contracts are presumed paritários e simétricos; minimum intervention and exceptional revision (snippet) — [Tribuna do Norte](https://tribunadonorte.com.br/colunas/clausula-contratual-limitativa-da-responsabilidade-civil/); [Planalto CC](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/2002/l10406compilada.htm) (statutory text, not fetched)
- STJ 3ª Turma (Feb/2024), by majority, upheld a limitation-of-liability clause between a multinational technology company and a Brazilian representative; the pre-agreed maximum indemnity prevails (snippet) — [STJ notícia 06/02/2024](https://www.stj.jus.br/sites/portalp/Paginas/Comunicacao/Noticias/2024/06022024-E-valida-clausula-que-limita-responsabilidade-contratual-entre-multinacional-e-representante-brasileira.aspx)
- Doctrine: a cap for culpa grave may survive only in paritário B2B contracts with objective definition of culpa grave; limitation clauses in software contracts discussed (snippet) — [Migalhas – software](https://www.migalhas.com.br/depeso/427638/clausulas-de-limitacao-de-responsabilidade-nos-contratos-de-software); [Migalhas – STJ e cláusula](https://www.migalhas.com.br/depeso/402200/stj-e-clausula-de-limitacao-de-responsabilidade-em-contratos); [Conjur 2024](https://www.conjur.com.br/2024-mai-08/clausula-de-limitacao-de-responsabilidade-protege-contra-riscos/)
- NBC PG 01: when a contador delegates part of the work, the technical responsibility remains his (snippet) — [Cosif NBC PG 01](https://cosif.com.br/publica.asp?arquivo=nbc-pg-01)
- E&O (Erros e Omissões) covers claims for failures in service, negligence, errors or omissions causing financial loss to third parties, incl. defence costs; offered for tech companies; no price figures published (snippet) — [Akad – RC empresas de tecnologia](https://akadseguros.com.br/seguros/responsabilidade-civil/profissional/empresas-de-tecnologia/); [Tokio Marine E&O](https://www.tokiomarine.com.br/seguros/seguro-responsabilidade-civil-profissional-eo); [Sompo E&O](https://sompo.com.br/produto/sompo-responsabilidade-civil-profissional-eo); [Allianz RC Profissional](https://www.allianz.com.br/seguros/grandes-riscos/linhas-financeiras/responsabilidade-civil-profissional.html); [Porto Seguro](https://www.portoseguro.com.br/seguro-responsabilidade-civil/profissional); [Fator Seguradora](https://fatorseguradora.com.br/responsabilidade-civil-profissional-eo/)

### Inferences
- Main damage scenario: a wrong suggested cClassTrib/CST leads to under-collection of IBS/CBS → auto de infração, multa and juros for the end client → the end client sues the accounting firm → the accounting firm seeks recourse (ação regressiva) against the vendor. The contract with the accounting firm should: cap liability (e.g., 12 months of fees), exclude lucros cessantes/indirect damages, exclude fines resulting from the client's own decision to adopt a suggestion, require human validation, set the reference date of the rules (the IBS/CBS tables — CST/cClassTrib — change frequently in 2026), and state the vendor does not perform escrituração, apuração, or assume responsabilidade técnica.
- A cap probably will not protect against claims by the end taxpayer (not a party) nor against gross negligence; hence E&O insurance plus good version-control/audit trails of which rule table version produced each suggestion.
- For SaaS, Termos de Uso + SLA; for done-for-you, a contrato de prestação de serviços with scope defined as "verificação técnica de consistência de dados" (not "auditoria"/"parecer").
- Some accounting firms are micro businesses; a court could apply CDC if the firm is found vulnerable (finalist mitigated theory) — lawyer to confirm; not sourced here.

- No public E&O premium data for small Brazilian tech firms; needs quotes via broker (Akad is digital and may give online quote).

## 5. LGPD: role, CPF in NF-e, small-agent regime, retention, international transfer (US AI APIs); sigilo fiscal/profissional

### Takeaway
When processing XMLs on behalf of an accounting firm, the vendor is most likely an **operador** (or suboperador — the accounting firm is itself usually operador of its client, the controller); must act on instructions (LGPD art. 39), needs a DPA, and is jointly liable if it breaches LGPD or instructions (art. 42). NF-e/NFS-e contain CPF/names/addresses of consumer recipients → personal data. As ME/startup it can use the small-agent regime (Res. CD/ANPD 2/2022: no mandatory encarregado if a contact channel exists; simplified records). Sending XML content to US-hosted AI APIs is an international transfer: since 23/08/2025 contractual transfers must use ANPD's standard contractual clauses (Res. CD/ANPD 19/2024) or another valid mechanism. Contadores may share with subcontractors under NBC PG 01 while keeping responsabilidade técnica, but sigilo obliges written authorization/confidentiality.

### Inferences
- Role chain: taxpayer (controlador of its customers' data on NF-e) → accounting firm (operador) → vendor (suboperador). The vendor needs a DPA/"Acordo de Tratamento de Dados" with the accounting firm stating: purpose limited to classification checking, instructions, confidentiality, security measures, incident notification, deletion/return after processing, and list of suboperadores (cloud host, AI API provider).
- Data minimization is the best mitigation: the IBS/CBS classification check needs item-level data (NCM/NBS, CFOP, CST, cClassTrib, values), not the recipient's CPF/name/address. Strip or hash `<dest>` personal fields (CPF, xNome, endereço) for B2C notes before storage and before any AI call → most data sent abroad would no longer be personal data, reducing Res. 19/2024 exposure. Emitter CNPJ data is not personal data, but MEI/individual-entrepreneur names/CPF may be.
- Using US AI APIs with personal data: need a valid art. 33 mechanism — sign/incorporate ANPD CPCs with the provider (or rely on provider's terms if they include the Brazilian CPCs; many only offer EU SCCs, which are not automatically valid in Brazil — confirm), disclose the transfer to the accounting firm, and prefer zero-data-retention settings. Brazilian region hosting (e.g., cloud in São Paulo) for storage reduces transfer exposure.
- Retention: keep XMLs only as long as needed for the service (e.g., delete after report delivery + short window); the legal 5-year guarda obligation for fiscal documents belongs to the taxpayer/accounting firm, not the vendor (confirm with contador; CTN 5-year decadência periods not sourced here).
- Sigilo fiscal (CTN art. 198) binds tax authorities, not private parties; the relevant duties for the vendor are contractual confidentiality + LGPD + the contador's professional sigilo (which he must preserve by imposing NDA on the vendor). Recommend an NDA clause and that the accounting firm's engagement letters with clients mention use of technology providers.
