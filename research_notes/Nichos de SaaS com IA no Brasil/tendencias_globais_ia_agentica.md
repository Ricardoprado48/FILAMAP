## 1. What do Gartner, McKinsey, IDC, Forrester, a16z, Sequoia, Bessemer and YC say about agentic AI adoption, spending and growth through 2030?

### Takeaway
Spending is exploding. Gartner puts 2026 worldwide AI spend at about $2.6-2.7T (+49.5% YoY), and IDC projects agentic-driven AI spend of $1.3T by 2029. Real production adoption still lags: McKinsey found only 23% of organizations scaling agents anywhere, and no function above 10%. Forrester expects 25% of planned 2026 AI spend to slip into 2027 as CFOs demand ROI. Investors (Bessemer, Sequoia, a16z) agree the big prize is the labor/services budget, not the software budget.

### Inferences
- There is a gap between spend forecasts, which run in the trillions, and scaled production (about 23% of organizations, 10% or less per function). Buyers want narrow agents with measurable ROI more than broad platforms, which favors vertical micro-SaaS sold on a clear outcome ("X hours of work saved" or "Y documents processed").
- Forrester's CFO scrutiny and Gartner's cancellation warning both reward products that price and report in business-outcome terms.
- Gartner's "agentic arbitrage" means that systems of record exposing APIs/MCP, plus agents operating across them, capture value. For a solo developer, building the agent layer over incumbent systems (ERPs, government portals, practice-management tools) is more realistic than building a new system of record.

## 2. Which vertical AI categories show the strongest traction and revenue, and why?

### Takeaway
Healthcare (clinical documentation and revenue-cycle admin) is the largest vertical by spend, and legal is the clearest breakout by ARR (Harvey, Legora, EvenUp). Accounting/finance back-office (Basis, FloQast) and insurance underwriting/claims (FurtherAI, Cytora) are accelerating. All of them replace document-heavy, language-heavy, billable professional labor, and pricing is anchored to labor or services costs rather than IT budgets.

Legal is about $650M (2025 data). — [Menlo Ventures](https://menlovc.com/perspective/2025-the-state-of-generative-ai-in-the-enterprise/); also [Menlo State of AI in Healthcare 2025](https://menlovc.com/perspective/2025-the-state-of-ai-in-healthcare/)

### Inferences
- The winning pattern is high-volume, document-heavy, rule-bound professional work where output quality can be checked. Examples: demand letters, bookkeeping/close, underwriting intake, prior auth/billing, mass litigation.

The tax-reform point is my inference; this research did not verify it.
- For a solo developer, the leaders (Harvey, EvenUp, Enter) target large firms with enterprise sales. The micro-SaaS angle is the long tail: small accounting offices, small law firms and brokers underserved by enterprise vendors, with self-serve pricing.

## 3. Which categories are saturated or commoditized?

### Takeaway
Generic customer-support chatbots, generic AI writing, "chat with your PDF", thin GPT wrappers and generic horizontal automation/"glue" tools are saturated or being absorbed by incumbents and model platforms (OpenAI AgentKit/Apps SDK, Salesforce, Intercom). The evidence is mostly qualitative and comes from secondary sources, but it is consistent.

### Cited Findings
- AI customer-support chatbots are listed among five oversaturated categories for 2026 because incumbents shipped them natively and collapsed standalone margins. The same source claims over 70% of founders build in markets past saturation. — [Preuve.ai](https://preuve.ai/blog/startup-ideas-2026) (low-quality/SEO source; directional only)
- The same source lists as least saturated: AI compliance tooling, vertical SaaS for "boring" industries, AI agent infrastructure, senior-care tech and fintech infrastructure. Their common moat is a regulated domain or a buyer that horizontal tools cannot reach. — [Preuve.ai](https://preuve.ai/blog/startup-ideas-2026) (directional)
- Customer-service AI is crowded, with incumbents at scale: Intercom Fin, Sierra, Salesforce Agentforce, Zendesk and others. Published per-resolution prices range from $0.50 (Quickchat) to $2.00 (Agentforce), which suggests price compression. — [Quickchat](https://quickchat.ai/post/ai-agent-pricing-models); [Intercom comparison](https://www.intercom.com/learning-center/ai-customer-service-agent-pricing-comparison); [Aissist benchmark of 18 vendors](https://aissist.io/industries/ai-agent-pricing-benchmark-2026)
- The 2023 wrapper era: many apps built on a prompt template plus a UI became obsolete when OpenAI absorbed their features at DevDay (Nov 2023). — [NoCodeWebsiteBuilder](https://nocodewebsitebuilder.com/blog/why-2023s-ai-apps-were-thin-wrappers-built-to-die) (secondary)
- OpenAI's AgentKit and Apps SDK (DevDay, Oct 2025) move into the "glue code" territory of Zapier/Make/n8n. Most at risk are lightweight ChatGPT wrappers, simple glue apps and generic automation suites without moats in data, compliance or UX. — [Josh Thompson](https://joshthompson.co.uk/ai/openai-agent-kit-apps-sdk-explained-automation-startups/); [AlternativeTo, Oct 2025](https://alternativeto.net/news/2025/10/openai-launches-interactive-chatgpt-apps-and-the-new-agentkit-toolkit-for-building-ai-agents/)
- Copilots, meaning tools that make a professional more productive, face commoditization as models improve. — [Sequoia](https://sequoiacap.com/article/services-the-new-software)

### Inferences
- Avoid: generic support bots, generic writing/marketing copy, generic RAG "chat with docs", horizontal workflow builders, and meeting notes (not researched here, but widely bundled by Zoom, Teams and Google).
- Open space is "boring" plus regulated plus local: the Brazilian tax/fiscal, labor (eSocial) and legal/cartório (notary-office) workflows, where global platforms lack local data, integrations and liability comfort.

### Gaps
- I found no rigorous, primary quantitative study of saturation (e.g., churn or pricing collapse by category). The evidence is qualitative or from SEO blogs.

## 4. What is the state of MCP adoption, and does it create product opportunities?

### Takeaway
MCP is now the de facto standard for connecting agents to tools. OpenAI (Mar 2025), Microsoft (Build, May 2025) and Google (Gemini SDK/Cloud) have adopted it, and it has been under Linux Foundation governance via the Agentic AI Foundation since Dec 2025. Security (tool poisoning, unauthenticated servers) is the major weak point. Opportunities exist in vertical MCP servers for local systems, managed/secure MCP, and "agent-ready" niche data products.

### Cited Findings
- 2025-03-26: OpenAI adopted MCP across the Agents SDK, and later the Responses API and ChatGPT desktop. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/26/openai-adopts-rival-anthropics-standard-for-connecting-ai-models-to-data/)
- May 2025 (Build): Microsoft announced native MCP in Windows 11 (MCP Registry, system MCP servers), joined the MCP steering committee, and integrated MCP across GitHub, Copilot Studio, Dynamics 365, Azure AI Foundry and Semantic Kernel. Nov 2025 (Ignite): MCP on Windows reached developer preview. — [Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/software-apps/windows-11/windows-11-model-context-protocol-mcp-ai-announcement-build-2025); [Windows blog, 2025-05-19](https://blogs.windows.com/windowsexperience/2025/05/19/securing-the-model-context-protocol-building-a-safer-agentic-future-on-windows/); [Windows Developer blog, 2025-11-18](https://blogs.windows.com/windowsdeveloper/2025/11/18/ignite-2025-furthering-windows-as-the-premier-platform-for-developers-governed-by-security/)
- Google: Gemini SDK MCP support (tools only; resources and prompts are not supported, per the source), Gemini Enterprise custom MCP servers, and remote MCP servers for Cloud Assist and Agent Platform. — [FastMCP/Gemini](https://gofastmcp.com/integrations/gemini); [Google Cloud docs](https://docs.cloud.google.com/cloud-assist/use-gemini-cloud-assist-mcp); [Gemini Enterprise help](https://support.google.com/g/answer/17106276?hl=en)
- Dec 2025: Anthropic donated MCP to the Agentic AI Foundation (a Linux Foundation directed fund) co-founded with Block and OpenAI. AWS, Google, Microsoft, Cloudflare, GitHub and Bloomberg are supporting members. MCP is Apache-2.0 licensed. — [Toloka](https://toloka.ai/blog/the-future-of-mcp-enterprise-adoption/); [WorkOS](https://workos.com/blog/everything-your-team-needs-to-know-about-mcp-in-2026)
- Ecosystem size (2026): about 28% of Fortune 500 have deployed MCP, 97M+ monthly SDK downloads, 5,800+ servers. CData estimates 30% of enterprise app vendors will launch their own MCP server this year. The Enterprise-Managed Authorization extension is now stable. MCP (agent-to-tool) and A2A (agent-to-agent) are expected to be used together. — [Search summary of Toloka / Digital Applied / Cloudmagazin](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/mcp-adoption-statistics-2026-model-context-protocol); [Cloudmagazin, 2026-07-06](https://www.cloudmagazin.com/en/2026/07/06/ai-integration-goes-enterprise-grade-model-context-protocol) (secondary; the Fortune 500 figure is unverified)
- Security: tool poisoning reportedly succeeded against major LLMs more than one-third of the time. About 7,000 internet-exposed MCP servers had been found by early 2026, roughly half with no authentication. OWASP lists tool poisoning as #3 in its MCP Top 10. Microsoft's June 2026 guidance treats tool descriptions as supply-chain assets. The NSA/DoD published an MCP security CSI (Jun 2026). In Apr 2026, researchers hijacked Claude Code, Gemini CLI and Copilot via PR-title prompt injection. — [Microsoft Security blog](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft-security-blog/the-state-of-mcp-security-in-2026/4531327); [CSA research note, 2026-07-01](https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-mcp-tool-poisoning-auto-execution-20260701/); [CSA, May 2026](https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-mcp-security-crisis-20260504-csa-styled/); [DoD CSI PDF](https://media.defense.gov/2026/Jun/02/2003943289/-1/-1/0/CSI_MCP_SECURITY.PDF); [Practical DevSecOps](https://www.practical-devsecops.com/mcp-security-statistics-2026-report/)
- OpenAI's Apps SDK (Oct 2025) is built on MCP, so ChatGPT itself is a distribution channel for MCP-based apps. — [FlowHunt DevDay 2025](https://www.flowhunt.io/blog/openai-devday-2025-apps-sdk-agent-kit-mcp-prompting-critical/); [MintMCP](https://www.mintmcp.com/blog/build-mcp-enable-apps-with-chatgpt)

### Inferences
- Product opportunities for a solo developer:
  - Vertical MCP servers wrapping Brazilian systems that have no official MCP. Examples: government fiscal/tax portals, NF-e/NFS-e, eSocial, court systems (PJe/e-SAJ), and popular Brazilian ERPs used by accountants. These could be monetized as paid, hosted, authenticated servers.
  - An "agent-ready" SaaS that exposes its core via MCP, reachable by customers' Claude/ChatGPT/Copilot users (distribution via the ChatGPT Apps directory or Claude connectors).
  - Security, governance and audit layers for MCP in regulated SMBs (LGPD, Brazil's data-protection law).
- Risk: generic MCP servers for popular SaaS (Slack, GitHub, Google) are built by the vendors themselves, which is consistent with CData's estimate that 30% of vendors ship their own. Only niche or local integrations stay defensible.

### Gaps
- I could not verify MCP registry server counts from an official source (the 5,800+ figure is secondary). I found no Brazil-specific MCP adoption data.

## 5. Known failure modes and risks

### Takeaway
Gartner predicts over 40% of agentic AI projects will be canceled by end of 2027 because of cost, unclear value and weak risk controls. It also flags "agent washing". Forrester predicts spend deferrals. Platform absorption of thin wrappers and MCP security are the other major risks.

### Cited Findings
- Over 40% of agentic AI projects will be canceled by end of 2027 due to escalating costs, unclear business value or inadequate risk controls (Gartner, 2025-06-25, based on a poll of 3,400+ organizations). Many use cases labeled agentic don't need agents, and most projects are hype-driven POCs. — [Gartner press release](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-25-gartner-predicts-over-40-percent-of-agentic-ai-projects-will-be-canceled-by-end-of-2027); [HPCwire/BigDATAwire](https://www.hpcwire.com/bigdatawire/this-just-in/gartner-predicts-over-40-of-agentic-ai-projects-will-be-canceled-by-end-of-2027/)
- Production inference costs can run 5-10x above pilot projections. — [Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/agentic-ai-project-cancellations-gartner-40-percent-2026) (secondary; not a Gartner quote)

## 6. Pricing models trending for AI agents

### Takeaway
There is a clear shift from seat-based pricing toward outcome, usage and hybrid pricing (platform fee plus per-outcome). This is driven by agents reducing human seats (Gartner's "agentic arbitrage") and by the services-budget framing (Sequoia's autopilots).

### Cited Findings
- Intercom Fin: $0.99 per resolution, with a monthly minimum of roughly $49.50 reported. — [Macha](https://www.getmacha.com/blog/intercom-fin-ai-explained); [Intercom](https://www.intercom.com/learning-center/ai-customer-service-agent-pricing-comparison)
- Sierra: outcome-based and custom-quoted, charging per resolved conversation, saved cancellation or upsell. Reported at about $1-2.50 per outcome with platform contracts from about $150K/year. — [CloudTalk](https://www.cloudtalk.io/blog/sierra-ai-pricing/); [Fin.ai on Sierra pricing](https://fin.ai/learn/sierra-ai-pricing) (competitor-authored; figures are estimates)
- Salesforce Agentforce launched at $2 per conversation and has since moved to consumption credits tied to conversations and actions. — [Quickchat](https://quickchat.ai/post/ai-agent-pricing-models); [Fin vs Agentforce](https://fin.ai/learn/fin-vs-agentforce)
- Seat-based SaaS revenue breaks when agents do work via APIs without humans opening UIs, and Gartner expects this to refactor how software is priced. — [CIO.com on Gartner](https://www.cio.com/article/4192242/agentic-ai-puts-234b-in-enterprise-saas-spending-at-risk-gartner-says.html)
- AI-enabled services price against legacy service providers (e.g., the cost of a paralegal or bookkeeper) and undercut them while keeping high margins. — [a16z (via search)](https://a16z.com/ai-workflow-productivity/); [Sequoia](https://sequoiacap.com/article/services-the-new-software)

### Inferences
- For a Brazilian micro-SaaS, a hybrid model fits: a low BRL base subscription (predictable revenue that suits SMB buyers used to monthly fees) plus a per-unit outcome fee (per document processed, per tax obligation filed, per petition drafted). Anchor it to the cost of an assistant's hours, not to software prices.
- Pure outcome pricing needs a measurable, attributable outcome and cost control over inference. Otherwise gross margin, cited at about 65% for vertical AI by BVP, erodes.

### Gaps
- I found no data on AI-agent pricing norms in Brazil or LatAm (e.g., SMB willingness to pay per outcome in BRL).
